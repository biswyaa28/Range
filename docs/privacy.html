Privacy policy

Personal OS · Effective 13 November 2026

Personal OS is a local-first personal dashboard. Everything you create is stored on your device first. Cloud sync, the AI assistant and health insights are optional features you turn on yourself. This policy explains exactly what each one handles.

Google account and cloud sync

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, Personal OS uses Firebase Authentication to confirm your identity and stores your data in Cloud Firestore under a document tree keyed by your Firebase user id (users/{uid}). Only you can read or write that tree; the security rules reject every other account.

What sync stores when you are signed in:

  • Blocks (your timetable), tasks, deadlines and notes, including their title, body, dates and completion state.
  • Your profile, home layout, app preferences and daily stats.
  • A device identifier and createdAt/updatedAt timestamps used to resolve conflicts between devices.
  • Deleted records are kept as small tombstones until they expire, so a deletion on one device is not undone by another.

Sync is last-write-wins. Google receives the data as our hosting and database provider; it is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than storing your account data. Personal OS does not run analytics or tracking SDKs.

AI assistant

The assistant is off until you add your own API key. When you send a message, the text you type, the conversation so far and the app data the assistant is allowed to read are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply.

  • Note text is included only when you turn on "Read your notes". It is off by default.
  • Notes marked private are never sent to the AI, even when note access is on.
  • Your API key is stored in the device's secure storage (Keychain on macOS, Keystore-backed storage on Android, the browser's local storage on the web). It is never written to Firebase or included in settings backups.
  • Requests go directly from your device to Google under your own API key and Google's terms.

Health Connect (Android)

With your permission, Personal OS reads only steps, sleep sessions and active calories burned from Health Connect.

  • Access is read-only. The app never writes health data.
  • Health data is processed on your device to show the Health section of Insights. It is never stored by the app, never synced to Firebase, never shared and never sold.
  • You can revoke access at any time in the Health Connect app. Because the app keeps no copy, there is no separate health-data record to delete.

Optional crash reports

Crash reporting is off by default. If you turn on Settings → Privacy → Send anonymous crash reports, the app sends exactly this to the configured crash collector:

  • the error name, message and stack trace, with emails, tokens, query strings and long identifiers replaced by [email], [redacted];
  • the app version;
  • the platform (web, macOS or Android).

Nothing else is sent: no notes, task or deadline titles, profile name, email, API keys, device identifiers, free-form text or screenshots. There is no third-party analytics SDK. Copy diagnostics copies the same redacted JSON to your clipboard without any network request. If the build has no collector configured (VITE_CRASH_REPORT_ENDPOINT), enabling the toggle sends nothing.

What we never do

  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • No advertising or marketing use.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No server of our own: the app talks to Firebase and, if you enable it, the AI provider you chose.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you clear it. In Settings → Data:

  • Clear data on this device removes the local copy only. If you are signed in, the cloud copy returns on the next sync.
  • Delete my cloud data permanently deletes every document under users/{uid} in Firestore, then clears this device and signs you out.

You can also delete your Firebase account and its data from the Firebase console. Backups you exported are plain JSON files under your control; delete them like any other file.

Children

Personal OS is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
